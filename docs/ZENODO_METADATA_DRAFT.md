## Description

This release provides research data and computational materials from a doctoral study of the terminographic representation of financial education across the Spanish-speaking world. The underlying corpus contains 18,933 entries from 57 institutional glossaries in 18 countries. The package includes a Total Normalized and Deduplicated Lemma Inventory (LTND) with 10,846 forms, a provisional Total Lemma Inventory Consolidated through Terminological Analysis (LTCAT) with 9,076 groupings and 11,516 denomination strings, a source manifest, reproducible analysis scripts, and selected SKOS/RDF modeling examples. The source definitions are excluded because they originate from third-party institutional resources with differing reuse conditions.
